What we collect and why

When you contact us or request a free check, we keep your name, business name, email and message so we can reply and do the work you ask for. Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying.

When you become a client, we keep contact and billing details for as long as we work together and then for 6 years for tax records. Lawful basis: contract and legal obligation.

Business research. To find businesses we might help, we look at public information such as Google Maps listings, business websites and the Companies House register. We only email limited companies and LLPs about our services, every email says who we are, and you can opt out at any time by replying "no thanks". We keep a record of opt-outs so we never contact you again. Lawful basis: legitimate interest.
