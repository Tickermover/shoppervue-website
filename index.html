About ShopperVue

ShopperVue is a small Port Talbot business run by Mousumi Kheto. We started it because so many good local firms, with great reviews from real customers, are almost invisible online: no website, an unclaimed Google profile, or old details that send people elsewhere.

We keep it simple. Fixed prices, plain English, and everything by email so you can answer when it suits you. We use modern tools, including AI, to work quickly and keep prices low, and a person checks everything before it goes live.

Our promises

  • We never promise "number one on Google". We promise the work, done properly.
  • No fake reviews and no paid reviews. Ever.
  • Your profile, website and web address stay yours.
  • Real photos of your work only, never stock or AI images on your Google profile.
  • Cancel monthly plans with one email.

Questions

Do I need to talk to anyone on the phone?

No. Everything is done by email, at a time that suits you.

Will you guarantee I appear at the top of Google?

No one honest can promise that. Google decides rankings. We make sure your listing is complete, accurate and active, which is what Google asks businesses to do.

Do I lose control of my Google profile?

No. You add us as a manager and you can remove us at any time. You remain the owner.

How do I pay?

By a secure card payment link after you've agreed the work by email. One-off jobs are paid on completion.

Which areas do you cover?

Mainly Port Talbot, Neath, Swansea, Bridgend and Llanelli, but because everything is online we can help businesses anywhere in Wales.
